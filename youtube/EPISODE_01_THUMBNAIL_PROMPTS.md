# Episode 01 thumbnail prompts

The image-generated concepts remain optional alternatives. The recommended
thumbnail was rebuilt as a native Excalidraw scene so its flat fills,
handwriting, rough strokes, and spacing match the video canvas. Its dark navy
background keeps it distinct from Episode 0. The editable source is
`canvas/thumbnail_ep1.excalidraw`, generated by

## Recommended — I built the smallest language model

This version is authored directly in Excalidraw rather than from an image
generation prompt. It uses the exact text “I BUILT THE SMALLEST LANGUAGE MODEL”
beside the two-name input, complete 4×4 probability table, and `ana` output.

## A — This table writes?

Create a 16:9 YouTube thumbnail in a tactile handmade editorial style: warm
cream paper texture, hand-painted navy lettering, simple ink outlines, pastel
lavender, blue, yellow, and mint accents. Small series label at top left: “LLM
FROM SCRATCH”; small “EP 01” badge. Huge left-side hook: “THIS TABLE WRITES?”
On the right, show paper slips “anna” and “ava” above an exact compact count
table with columns a, n, v, END and rows START, a, n, v. Values: START 2,0,0,0;
a 0,1,1,2; n 1,1,0,0; v 1,0,0,0. Highlight the a row. Draw an arrow to three
mint character tiles spelling “a n a”. Strong hierarchy, generous spacing,
accurate text, no face, no logos, no photorealism.

## B — Just 2 names? (concept prompt before the native Excalidraw rebuild)

Create a 16:9 YouTube thumbnail with a deep navy paper-textured background and
bold cream hand-painted lettering. Small series label at top left: “LLM FROM
SCRATCH”; small “EP 01” badge. Huge hook on the left: “JUST 2 NAMES?” with the
number 2 in bright mint. On the right, two cream paper slips labeled “anna” and
“ava” point into a small lavender box labeled “MODEL”, which points to a mint
paper slip labeled “ana”. Use a few violet arrows and blue underline strokes.
Simple, high contrast, readable at phone size, no face, no logos, no extra text.

## C — No neural network?

Create a 16:9 YouTube thumbnail on pale lavender paper with expressive black
brush lettering. Small series label at top left: “LLM FROM SCRATCH”; yellow “EP
01” badge. Huge hook on the left: “NO NEURAL NETWORK?” On the right, a paper
tag reading “current: a” is tied to a lavender bag labeled “NEXT?” containing
four paper tickets labeled n, v, END, END. A black curved arrow points to three
mint tiles spelling “a n a”. Editorial, hand-drawn, simple, high contrast,
readable at phone size, no face, no logos, no extra text.
