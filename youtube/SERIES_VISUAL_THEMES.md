# Series thumbnail themes

Count, Learn, and Attend use distinct backgrounds so viewers can recognize
which part of the series a video belongs to. Keep the hand-drawn diagrams,
Virgil font, and readable headlines as the common visual identity.

| Stage | Background | Main text | Accents | Diagram language |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Count · Episodes 01–02 | Navy `#0B1831` | Cream `#FFFBE9` | Blue `#88D0FF`, green | Count tables, character cards, probability bars |
| Learn · Episodes 03–08 | Warm cream `#FFF0D5` | Dark plum `#34233F` | Plum `#713C8C`, burnt orange `#C95724` | Adjustable numbers, dials, training loops |
| Attend · Episodes 09–14 | Deep plum `#321F47` | Cream `#FFFBE9` | Mint `#79DFC4`, coral `#FFAD91` | Token links, selective connections, attention patterns |

Learn means learning weights; count-based models also learn from data.
The Attend palette is reserved for that later stage. Existing Count assets
retain their established appearance.

## Count-to-Learn bridge

Use a narrow Count-colored strip on the left, transitioning into the warm
Learn background over most of the image. This signals the change of chapter,
rather than making the bridge look like another Count lesson. The bridge is
an orientation video; its thumbnail should not imply that it implements the
new model or demonstrates successful learned sharing.

Three editorial options:

- NEXT CHAPTER: count table to adjustable dial; the clearest transition.
- GIVE IT A DIAL: playful introduction to adjustable weights.
- CONNECT THE DOTS: connect what we have learned to the next stage.

Stage thumbnails use their own full background; the split treatment is reserved
for transition videos. Keep headlines concise and avoid implying that weights
alone solve the sparsity problem.
